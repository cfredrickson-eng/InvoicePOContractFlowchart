Invoice & Purchase Order Approval Routing

Sequential Approval — Non-Negotiable: Every invoice and purchase order must be reviewed and approved by each role in the chain before advancing to the next tier. No invoice or PO routes directly to the role with final approval authority. Every level signs off in order. Additionally, budget variance thresholds apply at each approver level — if the invoice amount exceeds budget by more than the role's variance limit, it must escalate to the next level regardless of the invoice's total dollar amount.
R&M, Contract Services & Turnover Invoices — Sequential Approval with Variance Thresholds
Admin, Contract Labor & Marketing Invoices — Sequential Approval with Variance Thresholds
CapEx Invoices — Full Chain Required for All Amounts
Purchase Orders — Sequential Approval Required at Each Tier
